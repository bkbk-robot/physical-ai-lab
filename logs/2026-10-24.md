# 2026-10-24 — Closed-loop grasping on SO-101 with SmolVLA

## What works now
- 99-episode single-task dataset (194,571 frames, 30 fps), absolute joint targets in degrees
- Pick-and-place inside the demonstrated workspace runs end-to-end, model-driven
- Two cameras (overhead C920 + wrist USB), 30 Hz control, re-plan every 10 steps (0.33 s)

## Improvements today
- 6-motor preflight before every trial: every run starts on a fully online arm
- Objective scorer replaces subjective judgement: counts objects inside the tray from the overhead camera
- Reset-with-retry: the arm always returns home (3 attempts), never left mid-air
- Denser closed loop: n_action_steps 50 to 10, visibly smoother motion

## Bottleneck identified
The default fine-tune uses train_expert_only=true, so the 500M vision-language backbone stays frozen: the policy grounds objects inside the demonstrated region but not outside it. Next run unfreezes the backbone (fits in 16GB at batch 6, ~4.8GB VRAM).
