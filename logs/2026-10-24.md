# 2026-10-24 — Closed-loop grasping on SO-101 with SmolVLA

## What works now
- 99-episode single-task dataset (194,571 frames, 30 fps), absolute joint targets in degrees
- **Pick-and-place inside the demonstrated workspace is working end-to-end**: approach -> descend -> close ->
  lift -> move to tray -> release, model-driven
- Two cameras (overhead C920 + wrist USB), 30 Hz control, re-plan every 10 steps (0.33 s)

## Improvements shipped today
1. **6-motor preflight** before every trial -> every run starts on a fully online arm
2. **Objective scorer** replacing subjective judgement: counts objects inside the tray from the overhead
   camera (no reference frame, robust to lighting)
3. **Reset-with-retry**: the arm always returns home (3 attempts), never left mid-air
4. **Denser closed loop**: n_action_steps 50 -> 10, motion is noticeably smoother

## Bottleneck identified (technical note)
The default fine-tune uses train_expert_only=true: the 500M vision-language backbone stays frozen, so the
policy grounds objects well inside the demonstrated region but not outside it. Next run unfreezes the
backbone (train_expert_only=false), which fits in 16GB at batch 6 (~4.8 GB VRAM).
