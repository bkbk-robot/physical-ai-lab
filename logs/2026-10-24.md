# 2026-10-24 — SmolVLA fine-tuning: what actually blocks grasping

## Setup
- Robot: SO-101, 6-DoF (pan, lift, elbow, wrist_flex, wrist_roll, gripper), absolute joint targets (deg)
- Cameras: overhead C920 (640x480) + wrist USB (640x480), 30 fps
- Dataset: 99 episodes / 194,571 frames / single task string (2 episodes excluded as corrupted)
- Training: SmolVLA fine-tune, batch 8, lr 1e-5, 60k-90k steps, checkpoints every 5k, RTX 5060 Ti 16GB
- Inference: 30 Hz, replan every 10 steps

## Result today
- Closed-loop success (objects placed in tray): 0-1 of 3 per run, over ~20 trials
- Policy-predicted action spans vs demonstrated spans:
  - lift 31 deg vs 158 deg
  - elbow 36 deg vs 163 deg
  - gripper 6.9 deg vs 32 deg
  => the policy emits a heavily averaged, low-amplitude action signal; it never closes the gripper enough
- Closed loop reached pan -120..-155 deg while the dataset pan range is -42..-3 deg (drift outside demonstrated region)

## Root-cause candidates tested
1. **Frozen vision backbone.** Default SmolVLA fine-tuning uses `train_expert_only=true`, so the 500M
   vision-language backbone never adapts to the new camera, desk and objects. Retraining with
   `train_expert_only=false` fits in 16GB at batch 6 (~4.8GB VRAM).
2. **Execution horizon.** `n_action_steps=50` = 1.7 s of open-loop execution per inference.
   Reducing to 10 (0.33 s) smooths motion, but does not change success by itself.
3. **Hardware.** The Feetech bus repeatedly dropped servos (IDs 1, 3, 6) and reported
   `Input voltage error`; several earlier "model failures" were measured on a partially offline arm.
   Added a 6-motor preflight before every trial.

## Tooling added
- Objective scorer: counts objects inside the tray from the overhead camera, no reference frame needed
- Preflight: all 6 motors must answer before a trial starts
- Reset-with-retry: the arm always returns home (3 attempts), never left mid-air
